The Six Cornerstones

Every NEO learning experience serves at least one Cornerstone — in what it teaches and in how it is taught. Not a bolt-on: woven into every session.

← Back to the curriculum

Connection

Relationships, communication, understanding self and others.

In mathematics: shared problem-solving, mathematical conversation, learning that starts from the learner's own world.

Movement

Physical engagement, embodied learning, the body as a site of knowledge.

In mathematics: manipulating, building, tracing, walking a number line — mathematics you can feel.

Creativity

Original expression, making, divergent thinking.

In mathematics: multiple routes to an answer, pattern-making, conjecture, elegant and surprising proof.

Reflection

Self-awareness, critical inquiry, metacognition.

In mathematics: "does the answer make sense?", reconnection sessions, reasoning made visible.

Rest

Nervous system regulation, recovery, the right to pause.

In mathematics: low-pressure practice, no-stakes exploration, pacing that respects the learner.

Nutrition

The body, food, health, physical self-care.

In mathematics: real-world contexts that connect number to daily life, energy and routine.

Cornerstone definitions from the NEO Teaching and Learning Policy. The mathematical interpretations above are working notes for the GreenPrint build and are open for revision.
