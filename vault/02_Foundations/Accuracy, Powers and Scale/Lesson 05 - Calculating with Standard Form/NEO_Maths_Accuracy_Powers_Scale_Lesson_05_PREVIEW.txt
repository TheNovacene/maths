NEO Mathematics - Accuracy, Powers and Scale
Lesson 05 - Calculating with Standard Form

This lesson uses the established NEO reference shell and includes:
- all six Cornerstones in the locked order;
- Why May the Factors Change Places? (regrouping, with the a x 10^n
  definition, 1 <= a < 10, stated precisely);
- Growing the Working: a chain-of-equalities builder - the learner
  constructs written working line by line, each line chosen from genuine
  alternatives and justified by a named fact (regrouping, index law,
  the definition, factorisation);
- Check This Working (Reflection): three handed-in chains judged line by
  line, including the matched-powers addition trap;
- Pose Your Own (Creativity): compose a calculation from tiles, predict
  whether its chain needs the rewrite line, write the chain by hand,
  then compare with a generated model chain;
- Cosmic Fact File Final Task with the full mastery rubric;
- six-question Practice Companion with chain-based worked solutions;
- Lines of Working Scratchpad background (plus the powers ladder);
- Learner and Supporting Adult Guidance;
- Reconnection Routes (index laws, writing standard form, multiplying
  decimals, dividing to a decimal) with an explicit return; and
- the universal independent worked-solution release rule.

Design note: the machine metaphor of earlier drafts was removed at the
Director's direction. The lesson teaches transferable mathematical
reasoning - every line justified, every chain checked against the
definition - so learners can approach non-routine problems from
understanding rather than procedure.
