NEO Mathematics - Accuracy, Powers and Scale
Lesson 04 - Write and Order Standard Form

This lesson uses the established NEO reference shell and includes:
- all six Cornerstones in the locked order;
- One Number, Three Costumes (standard-form convention);
- Micro-to-Macro Scale Explorer with predict-first "try one change" controls;
- Which Part Moves a Number Furthest? reflection;
- Design a Scale Postcard (Creativity), published in the Final Task;
- six-question Practice Companion;
- Powers-of-ten Ladder Scratchpad background;
- Learner and Supporting Adult Guidance;
- Reconnection Routes (place value, powers of ten, index laws, multiplying by 10)
  with an explicit return to the lesson; and
- the universal independent worked-solution release rule; and
- GreenPrint digit-shift language: the decimal point is a fixed separator,
  the digits move across the place-value columns.
