# NEO Mathematics Year 9
## Pythagoras and the Hidden Length - 10-session working unit v0.1

**Working unit status:** prototype for learner review and QA.

### Unit arc
1. The Harmony of Numbers - wonder and connection
2. What can three squares reveal? - theorem discovery
3. How can a theorem reveal a hidden length? - utility and method choice
4. Hidden Lengths Practice Studio - 45-minute progressive practice
5. Can we measure what we cannot reach? - modelling and application
6. How can one proof speak for every right triangle? - Perigal and proof
7. When harmony breaks - Hippasus, sqrt(2) and contradiction
8. Are Pythagorean triples random? - families and generating structure
9. Are squares really essential? - similar figures and A = k s^2
10. What makes Pythagoras more than a formula? - Evidence Studio

### Locked shell rules applied
- Driving Question and Stepping Stones at the top.
- Six explicit NEO Cornerstones in collapsible panels.
- Final Task panel with four-level rubric.
- Stable guidance / Scratchpad / Practice navigation.
- Explicit `What to do`, `What you are trying to notice`, and `What to carry forward` blocks.
- Nutrition explicit in every lesson.
- Layered Scratchpad with persistent background and dedicated Line tool.
- Practice Companion pattern: Socratic prompt -> Hint -> Check -> Worked solution.
- Conceptual dependency takes precedence over Cornerstone clustering.
- Full-construction containment is a visual QA gate.
