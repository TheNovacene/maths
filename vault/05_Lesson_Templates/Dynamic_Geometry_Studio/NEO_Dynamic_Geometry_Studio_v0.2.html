Dynamic Geometry Studio

v0.2 — the exact-construction toolkit: draggable points, authentic swing compass (with length transfer), quick circle, straightedge, perpendicular bisector, angle bisector, perpendicular & parallel lines, intersections, and length/angle measurement. Everything is dynamic: drag a base point and the whole construction follows.

Select a tool. Try Compass: click a centre, click a point to set the radius, then click again to swing an arc. Or press Demo: equilateral.

v0.2 notes: points auto-label A, B, C… · every dependent object (circles, arcs, bisectors, perpendiculars, parallels, intersections, measurements) recomputes live when you drag a free point · the Compass copies a length if you click a segment for its radius · click any object to select it (halo/glow) · Undo removes the last object. Still to come (Phase 3): protractor & bearings, transformations, animation, saved custom tools, and locus tracing.
